About Us

Queens Of Change Foundation is a nonprofit organization committed to empowering women and supporting underprivileged communities through education, leadership, and social development programs.

We believe every individual deserves equal opportunities to learn, grow, and build a brighter future.

Our Mission

Education

Providing educational opportunities and skill development programs for young women and communities.

Leadership

Encouraging leadership, confidence, and empowerment through mentorship and training initiatives.

Community Support

Creating impactful social programs that support health, equality, and community welfare.
